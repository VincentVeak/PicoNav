# The Big One: PNUT 2.0 and a Major PicoNav Update
{: .no_toc }

*October 4, 2026*

It's been a while since the last update, and a lot has happened. This release touches almost every part of the PicoNav, from the update tool on your computer to how your Pico thinks, dreams, and fights. Here's everything that's new.

<!-- Header image: PicoNav + PNUT side by side -->
![PicoNav and PNUT 2.0]({{ '/assets/images/updates/2026-10-04/header.png' | relative_url }})

---

## PNUT 2.0 (PicoNav Update Tool)

PNUT has been rebuilt from the ground up with a modern, refreshed design.

<!-- Screenshot: PNUT main screen -->
![PNUT 2.0]({{ '/assets/images/updates/2026-10-04/pnut-main.png' | relative_url }})

- **New look and feel.** A fully updated interface, now with **dark mode**.
- **Full save data editor.** Edit your deck, your Pico, your wardrobe, and your Deep Dungeon runs.
- **Built-in tooltips.** Hover over cards, merits, afflictions, and items to see their descriptions, pulled straight from the wiki and matched to your save data's version.
- **Color picker bound to your save.** Your PicoNav's colors now travel with your save data.
- **Automatic updates.** PNUT updates itself, and pulls the latest PicoNav firmware straight from GitHub.
- **Revamped backup and restore.**

## PicoNav Firmware

### Feels better in your hands

- **Button fix.** The input delay on the navigation buttons is gone. Mash away; it should feel much more natural now.
- **Animation skipping.** Skip repeat animations you've already seen in the menus. (Battles aren't included yet, but we're looking at it.)
- **Brightness settings.** Adjust backlight and LED brightness.
- **Timeout settings.** Choose how long the screen and LED stay on.

### Battery life

Several battery optimizations went into this release. In testing, a single charge went from 100% on September 22 at 9:40 PM all the way down to 0% on September 27 at 6:49 PM.

**That's just under 5 days (about 117 hours) on one charge.**

<!-- Optional: battery test photo or graph -->

### Ventures

Ventures now run on a **cooldown** instead of a charge up. You jump into a battle right away, and your PicoNav goes on cooldown afterward instead of making you wait first.

### Deep Dungeon, completely reworked

<!-- Screenshot: Deep Dungeon merit screen -->
![Deep Dungeon]({{ '/assets/images/updates/2026-10-04/deep-dungeon.png' | relative_url }})

Deep Dungeon is now a full roguelike run. The goal is simple: get as deep as you can.

- **Buy in.** Entering a run costs credits.
- **Gems are now archetypes.** Each gem changes how your whole run plays.
- **Card draft.** Draft your cards at the start of every run, so you have more say in your deck.
- **Bosses every 10 floors**, with boosted stats. The dungeon gets harder the deeper you go.
- **Merits.** Beat a boss to earn a passive buff for the rest of your run.
- **Afflictions.** Debuffs and negative statuses that can hit you or your opponent.

### Random events

Random events have been expanded to **26 in total**. Some are now contextual: they can offer you choices, and some will even force you into a battle.

### A Pico with a personality

<!-- Screenshot: thought bubble or dream message -->
![Thoughts and dreams]({{ '/assets/images/updates/2026-10-04/thoughts.png' | relative_url }})

- **Hidden natures.** How you raise your Pico slowly shapes who it becomes, and its behavior changes to match.
- **Thoughts.** Every so often, a thought bubble appears over your Pico. Press B to see what it's thinking.
- **Dreams.** Sometimes your Pico dreams while it sleeps. Check in on it at night.

### Activities menu

Farming has moved to a new **Activities** menu, which is where minigames and other activities will live going forward.

### Under the hood

- **Code refactor.** The firmware has finally been split up and reorganized into separate files, making it much easier to edit and iterate on.
- **Sprite compression.** A new compression method lets the PicoNav hold up to **9 times** as many sprites as before. More gear, more cards, more everything.
- **Bug fixes and quality of life changes** throughout.

## Heads up

This is a big update, and it's still a work in progress. Expect bugs, and expect things to change. If you run into anything, please let us know.
